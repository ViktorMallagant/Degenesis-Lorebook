Flayers

Redemption written in blood.

TERRITORYPilgrim Clan

KatharSys p. 239

THE PENITENT

Mercy Through Suffering

Bergamo’s scarred landscape attracts pilgrims and preachers seeking meaning in the apocalypse. More than twenty years ago, one preacher climbed the city wall and offered a message that held his listeners: mercy awaited those who acknowledged their failures and repented.

His demonstration was a whip across his own back. Horror and fascination became devotion, and the man known as the Penitent gathered followers who traveled from village to village, preaching repentance while scourging themselves.

The movement drew people with much to repent, including murderers and other outlaws. Shared guilt and public suffering turned them into the Flayers, a pilgrim Clan united around the Penitent’s teaching.

PROTEST

Saints of a Rival Faith

The Penitent teaches that redemption comes through blood rather than baptism with water. Admirers treat him as a saint, collect the Flayers’ blood, and mix it with cat’s bile as a supposed remedy for gout and infertility. Dead Flayers are mummified and placed in shrines.

This devotion troubles the Anabaptists. Driving the Penitent away would alienate the people who revere him, yet allowing his influence to grow strengthens a rival religious movement.

Inspired imitators spread versions of his gospel. Most lack his charisma, and the Anabaptists act against these lesser preachers, removing them from their pulpits and beating them out of town. The Penitent’s original following nevertheless remains powerful and is becoming a serious threat in Purgare.

ECSTASY

When the Whips Turn Outward

A Flayer procession tests both humility and modesty. According to local belief, the penitents only leave their ranks to strike spectators when a town is sinful. Their collective fervor can turn a display of self-punishment into an attack on the people watching.

Villagers often endure the blows without resistance. Fighting back would not erase the shame attached to their community, and neighboring settlements will soon hear that the procession found them wanting. The same men who proclaim remorse can therefore inflict fresh violence under the authority of repentance.
