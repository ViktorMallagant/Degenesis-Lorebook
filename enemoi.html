LEGACY

Heirs of the Convoy

Legends of the Convoy remember people who protected survivors as the world collapsed. The Enemoi carry a related tradition forward in immense, segmented UEO trucks, bringing weapons and justice to settlements and accepting knowledge as payment.

Their name has more than one proposed origin: some connect it to an enemy convoy, others to the Bygone wind gods called Anemoi. Neither explanation is certain. What survives without ambiguity is the Clan’s commitment to guarding a remnant of the old world.

THE CONVOY

A Fortress on Wheels

Five trucks form the Clan’s moving home: Aquila, Fornax, Sagittarius, Orion, and the Citadel. Painted blue and gray, each carries sixteen enormous wheels. The Citadel rises as high as a house and contains two interior levels.

When the Convoy stops, Marshallers guide the Drivers into a pentagon. Docking arms connect the trucks, supports anchor them to the ground, and steel plates and an overhead net complete the defenses. In roughly half an hour, the vehicles become a fortified compound whose entrance lies through Aquila.

Guardians watch visitors with rifles ready. The Enemoi welcome merchants and petitioners but remain wary, particularly when purchasing food: a truck was poisoned years earlier, possibly through Chronicler interference.

JUSTICE

Law in Exchange for Knowledge

Books, artifacts, and Stream glasses all interest the Enemoi. This appetite for knowledge has brought sanctions from the Chroniclers against settlements that trade with the Convoy.

The Clan protects communities against gangs and against incursions by Judges or Anabaptists. Invaders and would-be saviors receive the same scrutiny. The Enemoi uphold Bygone democratic principles, judging crimes when local Clans cannot safely confront the offenders themselves.

Their authority is meant to be temporary. When humanity elects a leader by majority vote, the Enemoi intend to serve that government. Until then, their own laws and defenses preserve the ideal.

TRUCKS

Five Parts of One Stronghold

Every vehicle has a distinct role, and training within the Convoy follows those responsibilities. Together, they maintain the Clan’s security, technical capabilities, mobility, and accumulated knowledge.

Aquila
Entrance, screening of strangers, and guard duty; the starting point for young Enemoi.
Fornax
Laboratories and workshops operated by the technical branch.
Sagittarius
Armory maintained and protected by the Armorers.
Orion
Hunting and reconnaissance vehicle with the strongest engine; training ground for Drivers.
Citadel
Data center of the Ancestors and home of the AMSUMO Acolytes.

ACOLYTES

The Heart of the Citadel

The Ancestors work at the Citadel’s monitors, sorting inherited data and adding new discoveries. Below them rest the Acolytes. Their hooded silhouettes conceal ceramic faces: they are AMSUMOs connected to the truck’s terminals.

These machines preserve the knowledge of civilizations and the locations of Bygone crisis centers apart from RG facilities. The Enemoi regard that inheritance as a key to humanity’s future, and would destroy it before surrendering it to the Cults.
