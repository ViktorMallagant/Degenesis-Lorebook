CLAN DOSSIER

Garganti

The herd remembers the law.

REGIONBorca / Pollen / Balkhan
TERRITORYIce Barrier
TECH LEVELII

KatharSys p. 229

DOMINATION

Life Beside Giants

The Garganti travel through Borca and Pollen on mammoths and spend the winter in the Balkhan. Around strangers, they conceal emotion behind scarves and protective goggles. Their reserve expresses a belief that joy and grief open the soul to outsiders, and it also suits the temperament of their mounts.

Mammoths react dangerously to sudden movement and loud noise. Even a child’s scream can startle one into sweeping its immense tusks toward the disturbance. Calm conduct is therefore learned early, and children ride quietly in panniers at the animals’ flanks.

Ancient commandments govern life with the herd and with other people. The elders say those laws and the first mammoth were given to their ancestors in the tower of creation. Bronze pendants bear a rune associated with the gates into the world: earth.

HOSPITALITY

The First Commandment

Peaceful visitors receive bread, ground salt, and water sweetened with roots. The Garganti consider that welcome their obligation, and expect an equivalent welcome when they approach another settlement. Refusal can be judged a breach of the Clan’s most important law.

A legend tells of Wroclaw denying the herd food and firewood. The Garganti entered the city by force, their mammoths destroying buildings as the Kagan advanced through Druschinnik arrows to the Piast’s palace. There, they allegedly discovered a secret whose written account remains sealed in the Kagan’s keeping.

The story ends with the Piast offering peace and asking forgiveness. Each autumn, the Garganti still rest outside Wroclaw before continuing to their winter grounds, and the city supplies what the herd needs.

IMPRINTING

Symbols the Mammoths Recognize

Traditional signs and sounds can quiet certain mammoths, lower their trunks, or make them trace patterns in the earth. The Clan’s strongest warriors claim responsive animals and train them for battle.

The strongest Garganti becomes Kagan. This leader interprets the laws and decides whether a settlement has failed its duty of hospitality. At the head of the herd, the Kagan can provoke his mammoth into an attack; the other animals follow its calls, turning the Clan’s advance into a mass of fur, flesh, and thunder.
