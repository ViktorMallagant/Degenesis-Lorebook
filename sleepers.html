THE FORSAKEN ARMY

SLEEPERS

Recombination Group · The Chosen

KatharSys pp. 271–275

CHOSEN

The Forsaken Army

Before the Eshaton, the Recombination Group selected exceptional people for a plan meant to outlive civilization itself. Intelligence, willpower, adaptability, and devotion made them candidates; conditioning turned them into instruments. Memories and loyalties were buried beneath layers of memetic programming, then the chosen entered cryostasis in underground Dispensers.

They were meant to wake into a world stripped of its old institutions and rebuild it according to Gerome Getrell’s design. Instead, many awoke late, early, or alone. Their civilization was gone, their instructions incomplete, and much of the infrastructure meant to guide them had failed. What remained intact was the conviction that they had been chosen to shape what came next.

Memetic Armor

Factual memories may be incomplete, but the conditioning and training intended to carry the plan forward survived remarkably well. Purpose remains long after context has vanished.

CASCADES

A Perfect Plan, Broken by Time

The original choreography called for a new cascade every century. Each wave would emerge, take key positions among the survivors, seed Getrell’s memetics into the culture, then die before the next cascade awoke. No two generations were supposed to meet.

The 216 virus, failing reactors, abandoned Dispensers, and centuries of neglect ruined that precision. Some 300s emerged after only 120 years while many 100s remained sealed away. Modern awakenings frequently bring partial amnesia and gaps in the locations of caches, facilities, and allies.

Some still pursue domination openly or infiltrate Cults and Clans from within. Others break their conditioning and become mechanics, sages, protectors, or rulers on their own terms.

Free Spirit Equipment

Any Tech V equipment can also exist as Free Spirit equipment at Tech VI. Damage or Armor rise by (2); other traits improve in a similar fashion, such as +2D. The equipment also gains Biometrically Encoded (8).

SYSTEM FAILUREThe plan survived. The timetable did not.

PREY

Ambrosia

Marauders crossed the centuries as well. They hunt Sleepers with ancient technology, drain their victims of blood, and consume it as “Ambrosia.” A Sleeper who abandons the plan therefore does not become invisible; they merely trade one set of pursuers for several.

POTENTIAL: REGENERATION
Gaping wounds close within hours, poisons are flushed 
out of the body, recovery from illnesses takes only a few 
days. Due to the nanites in their blood, Sleepers are blessed 
with an extraordinary metabolism. 
EFFECT: Every day, a Sleeper recovers (2) Flesh Wounds 
and (1) Trauma. If infected or poisoned, he gets +4S to his 
Action roll to resist the effects.

VARIANT: SOLDIER

Soldier

Violence lacks the subtlety of memetics, yet it is universally understood and works quickly. The RG Soldiers excel at violence. They break any resistance within seconds. They remove shamans and other elements acting pseudo-memetically from the Clan structure, thus enabling the Sociocybernetics to work without being disturbed.

VARIANT: SOCIOCYBERNETIC

Sociocybernetic

Sociocybernetics program people. They analyze the social construct of the Clan and modify it via the interaction of induced memes. In difficult cases, they mark unwanted elements and order Soldiers to remove them. They aim for total submission of the Clan.

VARIANT: HUNTER

Hunter

RG knew that no program runs without errors. The Hunters fight these errors. They monitor the memetic indoctrination of their cascade and bring deviants to the Sociocybernetics for reconditioning.

Their spies watch strangers in public spaces and report uncommon behavior. The Hunters follow up on every report, questioning villagers and listening to rumors and legends. If a Sleeper has broken their memetic chains and escaped to start a new life in the wasteland, the Hunters will find them.

Hunter Equipment

Encoder: Hunters wear torques or bracelets of pure white ceramic. These artifacts strengthen the nanites flowing beneath them in the Sleepers bloodstream, protecting the Hunter from Interferencers.

Interferencer: The weapon looks similar to a grenade launcher with a closed barrel. Instead of projectiles, the Interferencer fires a signal pulse on the nanites’ control frequency. A hit makes the target freeze. The nanites break from their cells and become visible as white dots in the victim’s pores. A strong hit makes Ambrosia run from the target’s eyes, nose and mouth as they choke on their own blood. The Intereferencer works on Sleepers and can also activate nanite cartridges.

VARIANT: INFILTRATOR

Infiltrator

The value systems of the Cults are codified and hard to manipulate, and an attack on the leaders seems inefficient. Due to established hierarchies, a successor would be ready in no time.

This is where the Infiltrators come into play. They copy the role of a low-level Cultist and work upwards through the ranks. Infiltrators are highly adaptive: they forge, deceive, and assassinate. As soon as possible, they direct the Cult into the next conflict. Their goal is subversion.

TACTICS

Cells, Watchers & Deviants

A Sleeper who breaks conditioning must stay beneath the notice of Marauders and loyal cascade members alike. Many build quiet influence in small communities, drawing on hidden RG caches to arm followers and secure themselves without approaching major metropolises.

Loyal Sleepers tend to preserve their old assault structure. Three or four Soldiers guard a Sociocybernetic who shapes a community. The Sociocybernetic directs Infiltrators wherever rival memetic systems are strongest, while Hunters operate at a distance—observing the formation and identifying ideological failure.

Formation

Soldiers break resistance. Sociocybernetics reshape society. Infiltrators turn established institutions against themselves. Hunters make certain the cascade itself does not deviate.
