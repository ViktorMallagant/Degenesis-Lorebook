CLAN DOSSIER

Corpse Eaters

Pain becomes hunger.

REGIONFranka
TERRITORYJanus Crater
TECH LEVELI

KatharSys p. 223

HUNGER

The Hunger Wakes

After an intense beam lit the night sky north of Briton, patrols began disappearing. Survivors encountered painted attackers who bit into defenders and tore flesh from the fallen, becoming still more frenzied when struck. Spitalians and Anabaptists answered the attacks with lethal force.

A captured Corpse Eater offered the Spitalians an explanation. Worms moved through its blood, and larvae clung to its neural pathways. Injury did not produce an ordinary pain response: the parasite turned that sensation into hunger. Torture only intensified the captive’s appetite.

THE WORM

A Clan Remade by Infection

The worm’s origin remains unknown. Its discovery in Clans from the Janus Crater suggests a connection to the Primer, but that connection is not established as certainty. Corpse Eater populations can disappear entirely and later return in their hundreds.

Killing every infected person does not end the threat. The parasite persists in soil, grass, and water; a passing Clan can become the next outbreak simply by drinking from a contaminated stream. The infection controls its hosts’ thoughts and behavior.

Those hosts believe that eating another person allows them to absorb ancestral knowledge, strength, and even an enemy’s hatred. Symbols still surface in their minds and appear in their body paint, remnants of a cultural identity being consumed by the worm.

PANIC

What the Worm Fears

Certain stimuli provoke terror in the parasite, which passes that fear to its host as an urge to flee. The beam that illuminated the Janus Crater caused such a panic, driving the Corpse Eaters away from the light.

Salt also frightens them. A change in the host’s internal chemistry may explain the response, though this remains a possibility rather than a proven mechanism. The fear could account for their failure to spread across the Stukov Desert.

NO PAIN

Hunger Beyond Injury

CLAN EFFECTIgnore Trauma penalties · +1 Ego per Trauma point

Corpse Eaters ignore all Trauma penalties. Each point of Trauma taken grants +1 Ego as the resulting hunger pushes the infected into a frenzy.
