CARRION OF THE GODS

MARAUDERS

Immortality · Ambrosia · Ruin

KatharSys pp. 277–281

MYTH & AGENDA

Gods That Refused to Die

Only a handful of Marauders are believed to remain, yet their traces cross centuries. Clans remember them as savage gods in rotting bandages, figures who appear beside forgotten laboratories, caches, and bloodlines before vanishing again for generations. Their interventions are rarely comprehensible: one settlement is guided toward a Bygone storehouse while another is erased for reasons no survivor can explain.

Chroniclers treat every report as a potential piece of a single puzzle. A burned village, a dome of impossible light, a dormant image wall awakening, or the sighting of a decaying immortal is catalogued and mapped. The underlying plan—if there is one—remains unknown.

What is certain is that Marauders hunt Sleepers and depend upon Ambrosia to hold back the centuries. The longer they survive, the more unstable the boundary between ancient purpose and madness becomes.

Bearing

Any credible Marauder sighting is valuable intelligence. The Chroniclers collect coordinates, descriptions, artifacts, and family histories in the hope that enough fragments will expose a pattern.

KNOWN MARAUDERS

Legends With Names

ASPERA THE ONE-LEGGED

Aspera

A lithe huntress with an immaculate, mask-like face and a silent mechanical leg. Over the centuries she has repeatedly allowed Chroniclers to find her, sometimes trading ancient knowledge and sometimes demanding sacrifice. No one knows what she is hunting; everyone who approaches her risks becoming part of whatever design she is pursuing.

ARGYRE THE VULTURE

Argyre

Britain is said to be his domain. Legends claim that he commands enslaved Sleepers fitted with a living yoke that punishes distance and disobedience. He has not been seen in centuries, but recent pillars of light rising from northern Britain suggest that something in the Vulture's land has awakened.

NORET

Gusev

Last seen walking the riverbed at the heart of Noret, Gusev passes among AMSUMOs without drawing their attention. Wild grain and impossible fruit now grow inside the Machine Men's deadliest zone. Whether that flourishing life is his work—or merely follows in his wake—remains unexplained.

THE BLACK GOD

Chernobog

Chernobog advances across East Borca behind an enormous Clan army. Praha Republika fell before him, and reports place him in Pest forcing his way through the Dushani sound mountains, destroying Aberrants, forests, and buildings alike. Whatever he seeks there has already cost thousands of lives.

ARIES THE RAM

Aries

Revered by Jehammedans after saving them from an Anabaptist advance, Aries is described as a walking catastrophe whose enemies collapse in blood and agony. He was last seen among his Arianoi on the Greek islands.

THE ICEBREAKER

Icebreaker

Once a year the Icebreaker crosses Pollen to demand Spitalian research on the Primer and Psychonauts. If satisfied, he leaves behind an artifact, formulae, or maps of subterranean growths; the Spitalians have learned not to discover what happens when he is disappointed.

TRIGLAW THE THREE-HEADED

Triglaw

Chroniclers classify him as a Marauder, while Clans across East Borca and Pollen worship him as the Horse Oracle. His movements connect the Alpine Fortress, Laibach, Cathedral City, and ancient networks of influence without revealing any consistent purpose.

POTENTIALS

Ambrosia & the Nanite Low

A

Ambrosia

Ambrosia keeps time from finishing what the Eshaton began. One point can immediately heal four Flesh Wounds or one Trauma, with no Action required. Legendary Marauders such as Argyre, Aspera, and Gusev can carry twenty to thirty points; lesser immortals may have only five to ten.

Every year and every wound thins the reserve. When a Marauder cannot replenish it, age and accumulated damage return all at once.

N

Nanite Low

When Ambrosia runs dangerously low, centuries of suppressed decay and panic break through. Marauders become desperate, irrational, and violently possessive of any chance to refill their reserves. Survivors of a Nanite Low often bear permanent physical damage or profound changes in temperament.

MYTHIC WEAPON

Soul Burner

Reports describe a biometrically encoded weapon that burns brighter than the sun and cuts through steel, concrete, flesh, and bone. Chroniclers have linked at least one sighting to Gusev, but cannot determine whether several weapons exist or a single Soul Burner changes hands.

VARIANT: THE CREATURE

The Creature

Wracked by ancient pain and repelled by ordinary human joy, the Creature flees into the wasteland and follows old Pathfinder routes between ruined RG facilities. It clings to its last spark of humanity while searching for injections and technology capable of keeping that spark alive.

The Guardian

Some Marauders recognize echoes of their own past in certain families. From the shadows they guide and protect these descendants, intervening with impossible knowledge and violence before retreating whenever rage threatens to overwhelm their restraint.

VARIANT: THE DIVINE

The Divine

Ancient, corroded by repeated Nanite Lows, and intoxicated by immortality, the Divine has accepted the role of god. Lackeys prostrate themselves around it, reinforcing a reality in which devotion is the only proper response and destruction the answer to dissent.
