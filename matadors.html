CLAN DOSSIER

Matadors

Every spectacle hides an opportunity.

REGIONHybrispania
TERRITORYWandering Clan
TECH LEVELII

KatharSys p. 235

BULLFIGHT

An Arena Between Enemies

Colorful carts carry the Matadors from the Castilian high plateau into African-held territory. In the arena, they fight bulls, presenting their own animals or facing those supplied by Neolibyans. Publicly, the spectacle preserves a fading Hybrispanian tradition and offers a day when enemies can sit together.

Neolibyan Consuls compete to host them in Granada, Seville, Malaga, and Gibraltar. Long festivals can please a restless population or provide an impressive setting for profitable negotiations. The Scourgers remain suspicious, watching for Guerrero movements around the performances, but the visible festivities offer little proof of a larger plot.

WELL PAID

Behind the Applause

The performance is also cover for spies and thieves. While crowds watch the arena, Matadors enter scriptoriums, Neolibyan strongholds, and Guerrero camps. Plans, business ideas, Balancer contents, and the locations of resistance forces all become merchandise for middlemen—or material for blackmail.

This was not their original purpose. They once rejected the war’s slaughter regardless of who suffered it. Their apparent revival of bullfighting won favor among Neolibyans but did not restore their standing with Hybrispanian relatives, who treated them with contempt.

In time, the other Matadors became their family. Detachment from both sides turned neutrality into a business: the same welcome that lets them cross borders also lets them gather information and sell it.

NIMBLE

Surefooted Above the Streets

CLAN EFFECT+2S to BOD+Athletics; +2S to escape a grapple

Matadors gain +2S to BOD+Athletics. When freeing themselves from a grapple, they gain +2S to AGI+Mobility. Their agility serves them on walls, ledges, and rooftops as well as in the arena.

AFFECTION

The Crowd Sustains Them

CLAN EFFECTRecover 1 Ego in a Round of crowd-winning risk

In each Round when a Matador wins over spectators through a dangerous maneuver, they recover 1 Ego. The maneuver must involve voluntarily rolling at least 2D fewer than they could otherwise use.
