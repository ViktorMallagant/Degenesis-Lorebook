RAPTURE

The Fleshformers

Primal

Body and mind collapse into a single adaptive system. Thought becomes tissue growth: muscle thickens, bones sprout weapons or dissolve into new joints, dead flesh is reinnervated, and lethal damage can be answered by another transformation. Biokinetics survive by physically becoming what the moment demands.

Focus

De-spored Biokinetics still value work, endurance, and the body over abstract planning. They see themselves as part of living nature and treat weakness or illness as failed development rather than tragedy.

Symbiosis

Solitary arthropods live in their folds and wounds, spin over exposed flesh, and lend venom to the Psychonaut. Older Biokinetics can carry whole swarms beneath hanging skin bags.

DOMINANT ATTRIBUTEBody
EARTH CHAKRAPandora Crater, Pollen
PLAGUESpiders · scorpions · centipedes

STRENGTHENING

Strengthening raises Body; each Overload also adds +1D to melee Skills for the combat. Strengthenings do not stack.

KNOWN PHENOMENA

Rapture Discharges

LEVEL 1

Lord of the First Plague

Calls and commands swarms of spiders, scorpions, and Rift Centipedes; swarm Strength scales with Overload and can attack or absorb damage.

LEVEL 2

Blossom

Sacrifices the Biokinetic to temporarily raise the level of a spore field.

LEVEL 4

Spider Bite

Grows venom cusps in the jaw; a successful bite delivers a poison whose Potency is set by Overload.

LEVEL 4

Nettle Hairs

Fires barbed, thorn-hard hairs that damage and hinder a target until safely removed.

LEVEL 5

Renewal

Sheds skin or even a trapped limb to escape bonds, with severe Flesh Wound or Trauma costs.

LEVEL 6

Absorption

Steals vitality from fertile surroundings to heal, leaving soil and life exhausted.

LEVEL 8

Fusion

Absorbs living biomass through sustained contact, eventually pulling the victim into the Psychonaut’s body.

LEVEL 10

Cusping

Seeds a Mother spore field with the dying Biokinetic’s tissue so that a new copy can be grown months or years later.
