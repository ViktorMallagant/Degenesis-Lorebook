CLAN DOSSIER

Cockroaches

Adapt. Breed. Survive.

REGIONBorca
TERRITORYProtectorate
TECH LEVELI

KatharSys p. 207

SHATTERED

Driven Underground

The Judges' purges shattered the old Clans around the Protectorate, burning breeding grounds and crushing communities until the survivors disappeared beneath the earth. Underground, the Cockroaches returned to the oldest law they knew: adapt, breed, and survive.

Every part of the Clan serves that survival. Warriors excavate new breeding places and gather meat. The old collect whatever food remains useful and are expected to endure without becoming a burden. Readers study the movements of vast cockroach populations, treating the swarms as omens that tell the Clan when to remain hidden and when to rise.

The Brood

For the Cockroaches, numbers are strength. Individuals are expendable when the survival of the swarm is at stake, and every generation is raised with the expectation that the Clan must emerge stronger than before.

RISEN

The Omen of Praha

Praha's fall was read as the sign the readers had been waiting for. Cockroaches and neighboring insect Clans poured from hidden warrens, attacking Judge convoys and erupting from old tunnels beyond Protectorate walls. One of those underground routes surfaced inside Justitian itself.

The Judges stopped the first assault at heavy cost, but the greater wound was certainty: the Clans they had driven into the dirt were no longer content to remain there.

PHOTOPHOBIC

The Hunted Become Hunters

Cockroach warbands move through tubes, buried infrastructure, and overgrown ruins, striking in masses and scattering when the odds turn against them. Their breeding places lie deep in the intestines of Bygone cities where daylight and enemy patrols rarely reach.

The dead are carried back below. During the rite of passing, warriors roast and eat a piece of the fallen so that the dead sibling's soul remains within the swarm. Bones become weapons, powders, and talismans; splinters are driven through living skin to keep the beast within awake.

Swarm Warfare

A Cockroach does not need to survive for an attack to succeed. The Clan measures victory in the survival and fury of the whole brood rather than the safety of any single warrior.

KINGS

Blood, Caste & Strength

Gatherers, readers, warriors, and kings belong to castes defined by blood and ancestry. None carry more terrible prestige than the Cockroach Kings: malformed colossi born from ancient family lines and famed for extraordinary strength.

The Kings are chained in the deepest vaults and treated more like breeding beasts than rulers. Chosen women are sent to them in the hope of producing children marked by their bulk and power. Those children enter the warrior caste, raised for fighting, looting, and destruction as the purpose of their lives.

DERANGED

Death Feeds the Swarm

CLAN EFFECT+1 Ego to every witnessing Cockroach

When a Cockroach dies in battle, every Cockroach who witnesses the death gains +1 Ego. The surge can temporarily carry Ego above the character's normal maximum. A fallen warrior is therefore not merely a loss; the death becomes a fresh provocation, driving the survivors deeper into the Clan's cycle of fury and revenge.
