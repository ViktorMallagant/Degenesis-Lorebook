CULT DOSSIER

Anabaptists

Workers, warriors, mystics, and torchbearers struggling to reclaim Paradise from the Demiurge.

Leaders

8 Baptists: Altair, Amos, Nuntius, Orphid, Trachees, and 3 unnamed Baptists.

Major Locations

Cathedral City · Justitian

Creation Skill Bonuses

+1 maximum: AGI+Mobility, BOD+Force, BOD+Melee, INT+Legends, PSY+Faith or Willpower.

Briefing

Torchbearers in Paradise

The Anabaptists believe the world is Paradise corrupted by the Demiurge. Matter is imperfect, the body is a prison, and the divine Pneuma within humanity longs to escape it. Psychonauts and the Sepsis are proof that the Demiurge still poisons creation. Against this corruption, the Anabaptists offer work, faith, community, and war.

Ascetics reclaim poisoned soil, restore fields, build irrigation, and feed settlements. Orgiastics defend those gains with swords, flails, Spitfires, and overwhelming violence.

Rebus and the Neognosis

The Cult began with Rebus the Baptist, whose search led him to the Neognosis: divine spirit opposed to corrupt matter. God is Pneuma while the Demiurge is bound to the physical world. Humanity is divine breath trapped within flesh. Rebus armed farmers and taught them to reclaim poisoned land. In 2148 his followers seized the cathedral that became Cathedral City. Two centuries later, Psychonauts seemed to vindicate his prophecies.

Two Hearts

Ascetics seek transcendence through labor, deprivation, meditation, and self-denial. Orgiastics confront carnality through excess, violence, passion, and battle. Both believe the Pneuma must triumph over flesh.

Baptism and the Seed

Newcomers are Touched. Those who remain are baptized, take the nose ring symbolizing bondage to the body, and receive three tattooed marks upon the forehead. Devoted Anabaptists search for the Seed, the first spark of divine insight; the Council of Emanations judges visions, voices, intuitions, and other emanations.

Hierarchy and Revelation

Ascetics may become Elysians; Orgiastics may become Furors. Emissaries carry the Baptists' will, Counselors lead the Council of Emanations, and eight Baptists lead the Cult. Sublimes stand outside normal progression; some become Acherons and use forbidden powers derived from Burn and Sepsis.

The Four Rivers

Elysian oils are named for Perat, Hiddekel, Gehon, and Pischon. Two forbidden rivers also exist: Styx and Acheron, both produced with Burn.

War for Paradise

Psychonauts are Spawn of the Demiurge; spore fields and Sepsis are wounds in Paradise. A common enemy supports alliance with the Spitalians. Apocalyptics are despised for Burn, Chroniclers are ancient enemies, and the deepest hatred is reserved for the Jehammedans.

Paradise and Power

Aqueducts bring water, fields produce food, labor restores ruined land, and fighters defend communities. This success also makes the Cult a political power.

The Anabaptist Conflict

Anabaptists are builders and destroyers, mystics and laborers, healers and berserkers. Each is asked to cultivate a field, a community, or the Seed within—and decide what must be uprooted so Paradise can grow.
