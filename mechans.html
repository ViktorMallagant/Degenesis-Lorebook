CLAN DOSSIER

Mechans

The tunnels belong to them.

REGIONBorca
TERRITORYRamein Region
TECH LEVELIV

KatharSys p. 211

CAULDRON CITY

The Mechanist’s Inheritance

Engineer Jewgeny Kagarast, remembered as the Mechanist, answered the collapse of society with the Cauldron Cities. Buried furnaces and heating pipes sheltered survivors from the cold and from attackers. Different cities supplied metalworking, steam engines, and tunnelers to a network intended to prosper through cooperation.

His workshops expanded underground as he developed turbines and weapons. After his death, however, his followers fought, the network fragmented, and technical understanding faded into reverence for supposed miracles. The Mechans became the surviving custodians of that inheritance.

TECHNOCRACY

Knowledge Made into Authority

The Mechans forbade the sharing of their secrets and styled themselves as priests. Steam-powered bolt guns, the Pneumo Hammers, went to selected warriors of defeated Clans, establishing the Pneumancers as an armed caste.

Ruling so many enemies exhausted the Clan’s resources. The Mechans elevated the Taunar into a governing caste and founded Nullpellia, binding rulers, priests, and warriors into a system of control and punishment. Prosperity did not prevent the Taunar from growing ambitious or the Pneumancers from becoming independent.

A falling star destroyed the Taunar palace. The Mechans proclaimed the catastrophe a divine endorsement of their own rule and resumed their campaigns with Pneumancer support.

THE TIME OF REVENGE

Nullpellia Falls

Continual conquest weakened the Pneumancers and drove the persecuted Phosphorites to revolt. Nullpellia could not withstand the resulting violence. Even an alliance with the Judges failed to save it, and the Pneumancers’ betrayal sealed the city’s fate.

The surviving Mechans retreated into the Mechanist’s buried works. There they found forgotten plans, ancient passages, and halls containing RG-marked Freon tanks. Their defeat uncovered the means for another attempt at power.

TACTICS

Control Beneath the Surface

Modern Mechans use their tunnel network to appear unexpectedly throughout the Ramein region. Raids and child abductions force families and settlements to accept their authority.

Their Freon ray guns resemble Chronicler weapons. The Mechans believe the Chroniclers built their influence on stolen or inherited plans from the Mechanist, and hate the Cult for that perceived appropriation. Technology remains their instrument of domination rather than a promise of shared prosperity.
