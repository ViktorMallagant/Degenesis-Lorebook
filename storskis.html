CLAN DOSSIER

Storskis

Steam carries the family forward.

REGIONBorca / Pollen
TERRITORYEast Borca to Pollen
TECH LEVELIV

KatharSys p. 221

IRON

A Railway Inheritance

Jegor Storski repaired a steam locomotive in an East Borcan ruin and drove it along a neglected line, scattering debris and trees as astonished survivors emerged to watch. That journey became the founding story of a Clan whose descendants have spent centuries reclaiming a branching railway network.

Armored locomotives now carry mail, weapons, food, and merchants between settlements. Steel shutters protect windows and wheels, while fighting platforms, coal catapults, and steam throwers help the crews defend their moving homes.

FAMILY

Building a Place in the Clan

Hard-working outsiders can marry into the Storski family, but a train cannot grow without limit. When space and traction run short, young members leave to earn a locomotive of their own.

Many begin as Charcoal Burners beside busy roads. Coal sold to relatives pays for iron and smithing tools; workshops beside railway sidings gradually produce an engine and wagon. Years or decades may pass, and a settlement can grow around the work yard before its builders finally depart as merchants.

Family lines respect one another, but power still determines precedence. The stronger train claims the right of way and the final word. Races on parallel tracks settle rivalries and demonstrate improvements to engines or gearboxes.

SIGNALS

Messages Through Iron

The Storskis transmit pulses along the rails to stations many kilometers away. Warnings about hostile Clans and calls for assistance pass through the same infrastructure that carries their trains.

The Needle Towers Chromium and Iridium have adopted these lines for communication. Payments in weapons and slaves support track maintenance and the preparation of connections that allow a carrier wave to bear their messages. The alliance brings new technology into a transport network built on coal and iron.

THE CARBON

A City That Never Stays

More than five hundred meters long, the Carbon runs between Chromium and Iridium. Extra-wide, multistory cars contain workshops, a casino, brothels, and even a small farm for tomatoes and spices. At each stop, its inhabitants and traders spill into the surrounding settlement before the train gathers passengers and moves on.

Intercoms broadcast prices and the Engineers’ songs. Several cars remain closed to outsiders, and three are forbidden even to Storskis. Their solar panels and satellite dishes contrast with the rest of the train.

Rumors place fugitive Chronicler Zeros in these restricted cars and suggest that a Needle is establishing an East Borcan Cluster aboard the Carbon. The suspected project would maintain contact with Chromium and Iridium through the railway network.
